# Source Line congestion diagnostics for the 24-hour synthetic OPF

**Scope:** Original synthetic-source transmission Line IDs, 10 source-model buses, 9 source-model Line records, 24 assumed hours, 3 artificial thermal rating levels and 2 assumed solar capacity levels. This is a **synthetic method-validation experiment**, not real Palo Verde transmission congestion or measured power-system economics.

## Added diagnostics

The file `outputs/synthetic_24h_figures/synthetic_24h_per_line_metrics.csv`, when generated by a **successful** [24-hour CI solve](../.github/workflows/source-topology-ten-bus-24h-sensitivity.yml), contains one record per Line × scenario (9 × 6 = 54 rows):

- `source_line_id`: immutable original network model ID.
- `congested_hours`: number of the 24 synthetic snapshots where modeled absolute Line flow is within 0.15 MW of its **hypothetical** rating. This is a tolerance-based near-binding indicator, not an operator event.
- `peak_abs_flow_mw`: maximum of modeled absolute sending-end Line power over 24 hours.
- `sum_abs_flow_mwh`: sum of absolute modeled Line MW across 24 unit-hour snapshots. Not measured transmitted energy.
- `sum_absolute_nodal_price_spread_units_per_mwh_hours`: sum over 24 synthetic hours of the absolute optimized nodal marginal-price difference between the two original source Bus endpoints, calculated from PyPSA dual prices. This is a **model price-separation diagnostic** in arbitrary cost units, not a validated tariff, merchant transmission profit, or independently confirmed value of new Line capacity.

A positive modeled price difference may be associated with a binding modeled constraint. It must **not** automatically be called a capital investment benefit, because real expansion costs, contingency limits, N-1 security, market prices, AC reactive flows, losses, operating conditions and actual physical POI identities are absent.

## Consistency gate

For every scenario, the sum of the 9 source Line congestion-hour counts must equal the sum of the 24 hourly counts already stored in the original JSON. The workflow also rejects nonfinite or missing dual-price data and validates that the source Line IDs and selected source bus endpoints remain consistent. A labeled source-Line congestion chart is exported as `synthetic_24h_per_line_congestion.png`.
