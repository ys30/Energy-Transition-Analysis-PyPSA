## Nonnegotiable method constraints

1. Use only EIA generator sheet **Operable**, with strict `Status='OP'` (operating). Do not add `Proposed`, `Retired and Canceled`, standby or out-of-service units. Filter only onshore wind `Prime Mover='WT'`, `Energy Source 1='WND'`; and utility PV `Prime Mover='PV'`, `Energy Source 1='SUN'`. Any unrepresented class is not silently reclassified.
2. `Plant Code` must exactly match a unique EIA plant record; confirm state and county between generator and plant where present; reject state/county conflicts. Each `(Plant Code, Generator ID)` must be unique. Use exact normalized county names within same `STATE` mapped to Census 5-digit FIPS; log failures with unmatched MW rather than dropping them.
3. EIA Form 860 nameplate generator MW uses AC according to its reporting instruction (see https://www.reginfo.gov/public/do/DownloadDocument?objectID=126696201). This is a cross-section of 2025 reported operating equipment only. ReEDS wind resource **potential** is MW_AC while source solar PV **potential** is MW_DC: only compare **within technology** and normalize each source's county MW into fractions; do not add physical AC and DC capacities or infer annual production.
4. Compute `w_EIA,c=EIA_2025_operating_nameplate_AC,c / sum_EIA_nameplate_AC` and `w_ReEDS,c=ReEDS_county_developable_source_potential,c/sum_ReEDS_potential`. Compare county total variation distance `TV=.5 sum_c |w_EIA,c - w_ReEDS,c|` using union of county support; report map coverage and any observed fleet MW in counties with zero ReEDS resource potential. Compare effective county count `1/sum_c w_c^2`.
5. **Neither quantity is projected 2030 installed capacity**, and neither directly estimates the operational effect of different deployment siting on the 2012 original source HDF5 weather shapes. A separate county-class profile replay and strict source time/AC normalization is required for any new LP sensitivity.
6. On zero or insufficient 2025 reported operating capacity in a state/technology, emit missing or explicit no-fleet coverage status rather than fabricate a weight vector. Excluded geographic records affect coverage denominators, not the claimed matched geographic share.

## Intended research interpretation

The current Paper S common-AC source-weather LPs, 216 numerical source-hour comparisons and 108 battery cost/efficiency scenarios have authentic ReEDS resource inputs but *hypothetical geographical deployment weights*. This EIA 2025 external check is a **capacity-siting representativeness diagnostic**. A small TV could show potential shares resemble 2025 observed fleet distribution at county level; a large TV would indicate strong geographic discrepancy. Neither is a validated forecast of grid value, a true 2030 installed-fleet model, or certified 2012-to-hour-ending energy conversion.

Keep the proposed Paper S manuscript addendum and PR #2 in Draft until real-fleet spatial benchmark has passed source-join QA and actual county-reweighted energy profiles are computed, or explicitly mark spatial validity as a limitation. **ReEDS wind/PV source profiles are 2012 instantaneous point CF with reference-year exclusion of 31 December; the 216-LP numerical approximation sensitivity does not independently certify hourly physical generation.**
